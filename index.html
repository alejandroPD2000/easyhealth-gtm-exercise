Northwind Health Plan
1Signal
Every October CMS publishes Star Ratings, per plan and per measure. Northwind lost its bonus; weak on screenings.
WHY Public, and it says who's hurting now.
2Rank
500 plans: 50 one-to-one, 150 by group, 300 automated. Re-ranked weekly by signals.
WHY Same effort on 500 = shallow effort on all.
3Reach
AI drafts the opener from Northwind's own scores. The AE reviews every one and sends it to the quality/Stars owner.
WHY One wrong number burns a top-50 account.
4Qualified demo
Right account · Director+ owner attends · concrete target named · dated next step. Scored from the call, not by hand.
WHY Booked isn't qualified.
